The idea: turn scattered weekly updates into a clear view of goals, progress, and follow-up work.

What does it do?

Weekly Pulse helps review your information and draft a summary. You remain in control of checking it before using or sharing it.

✦

Gather updates

Bring together work updates, personal notes, and meeting details you provide.

⌘

Organize by topic

Group work by project, outcome, next step, and items that need follow-up.

✓

Draft for review

Keep the summary concise, retain source references, and flag missing details.

From notes to weekly summary

A five-step workflow that keeps the results easy to verify.

STEP 01

Collect information

Receive the content and date range to summarize.

STEP 02

Find key updates

Identify outcomes, progress changes, plans, and issues.

STEP 03

Cross-check sources

Compare details and flag missing or inconsistent information.

STEP 04

Group by project

Separate completed work, ongoing tasks, and follow-ups.

STEP 05

Draft the summary

Create a structured draft for you to review and finalize.

A clear, practical weekly summary

Adapt the structure to your class or workplace requirements.

↗

Weekly outcomes

◫

Work in progress

!

Issues to watch

Blockers, dependencies, and items that need confirmation.

⌁

Sources

Notes, documents, and original links provided by the user.

Guiding principle: never invent progress, figures, or conclusions when the source is missing. Review the draft before using it.
